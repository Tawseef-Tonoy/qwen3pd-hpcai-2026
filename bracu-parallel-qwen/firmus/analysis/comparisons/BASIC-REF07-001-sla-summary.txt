Findings:

1. None of the exploratory REF07 sweep points achieved GFR >= 0.90.

2. C48 produced the highest SLA-qualified throughput among tested points:
     6.6213 good req/s
     1324.26 good output tokens/s

3. At C48, TTFT is the effective SLA bottleneck.
   ITL passed for 534/535 completed requests.

4. C56 increased raw offered concurrency but reduced SLA-qualified
   throughput relative to C48 and introduced additional ITL failures.

5. C8 exhibited six grace-period cancellations correlated with six
   requests resident in the P/D bootstrap/transfer path at cancellation.

6. The visible KVTransferError/AbortReq messages following C8 timeout
   are treated as consequences of cancellation, not proven causes of
   the preceding stall.

7. REF07 uses exploratory 20/60/15 timing. Therefore these results do
   not establish performance under the official 60/120/30 benchmark.
